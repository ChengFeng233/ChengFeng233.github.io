I’m Gengyuan Hu. My work spans **multimodal model evaluation** and **applied computer vision**. I’m interested in how models use information across time, from reasoning about changes between clinical exams to interpreting camera observations in robot vision.

In [ELTLM]({{ '/projects/eltlm-bench/' | relative_url }}), we study whether large multimodal models can tell what changed across clinical exams and reason about disease progression. The paper appeared in **Findings of ACL 2026**. I also maintain a [robot-vision codebase]({{ '/projects/robogame-vision/' | relative_url }}) covering camera calibration and AprilTag-based localization for USTC ROBOGAME 2025.
